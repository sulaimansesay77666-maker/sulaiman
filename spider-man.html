What is Spider-Man?

Spider-Man is a fictional superhero created by Marvel Comics in 1962. He is one of the most famous and successful pop culture characters in the world..

What is Spider-Man about?

Spider-Man is a story about the heavy cost of doing the right thing. It follows an ordinary guy trying to balance a messy personal life with the duty of protecting his city.

Why I Like Spider-Man

I like Spider-Man because he is just like us. He is the most human and down-to-earth superhero ever made.
